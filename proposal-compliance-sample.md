# Proposal Compliance Matrix

This fictional work sample shows how I would track customer requirements
from the proposal outline through final review.

| ID | Customer requirement | Where the proposal answers it | Evidence to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Explain who reviews and approves changes to work instructions. | §2.1 Review and approval | Approval workflow showing who signs off |
| R-02 | Explain how technicians access the current revision. | §2.2 Release and distribution | Sample document register showing revision and effective date |
| R-03 | Explain how obsolete copies are removed from use. | §2.3 Obsolete document control | Sample record showing withdrawal of a superseded copy |

**Pink Team:** Check that the outline covers all three requirements and identify
who can supply the evidence.

**Red Team:** Read the draft as an evaluator. If §2.3 says only “obsolete copies
are removed,” ask who removes them and how removal is recorded.

**Gold Team:** Verify that the final draft resolves that comment and that
the supporting evidence matches the procedure described.

About Me

Born and raised in Florida, I'm a technology and engineering documentation professional with more than 15 years of experience spanning aerospace, defense, and electronics manufacturing.

My interest in technology and problem-solving led me to earn two bachelor's degrees from Eastern Florida State College, one in Computer Information Systems Technology with a specialization in Data Science and another in Business with a focus on Organizational Management. I'm currently pursuing my Master of Science in Data Science at Walden University.

Outside of my professional and academic pursuits, I have a deep appreciation for art, nature, and the world around me. I enjoy visiting museums and art galleries, exploring my own creativity through painting and drawing, and spending time outdoors. Gardening, birdwatching, and observing Florida's diverse wildlife are among my favorite ways to unwind. I've always had a special affection for animals and find inspiration in the natural beauty of my home state.

The beach and ocean have always held a special place in my life. As a former avid surfer, I spent countless hours enjoying Florida's coastline and developed a lasting connection to the water. Although I no longer surf as frequently, my appreciation for the ocean, coastal environments, and the sense of peace they bring has never faded.

Whether I'm exploring a new artistic technique, discovering something fascinating in nature, or learning a new technical skill, curiosity has always been a driving force in my life.
